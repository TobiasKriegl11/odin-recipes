Spaghetti Carbonara

Carbonara is a traditional Roman pasta dish.

It is made from spaghetti, eggs, Pecorino Romano (the cheese), guanciale (cured pork), and black pepper.

It has a rich creamy sauce, created by emulsifying raw eggs and cheese with pasta water.

This dish is served al dente, topped with crispy guanciale.

Ingredients

  • Pasta: 400g of high quality Spaghetti.
  • Guanciale: 150g. This is the original choice, pancetta is a common substitute.
  • Eggs: 4 egg yolks & 1 whole egg. The yolks give the rich, golden color and creamy texture.
  • Pecorino Romano: 50-75g, freshly grated. The saltiness is essential for the dish.
  • Black pepper: Season generously and to taste.
  • Salt: For the pasta water (use less than normal, as the meat and cheese are already very salty).
  • Basil:For serving the pasta.

Steps

  1. Preparation:

    Boil Water: Bring a large pot of waer to a boil. Add a small pinch of salt.

    Prepare Guanciale: Remove the rind and cut it into small cubes and strips.

    Make the Cream: Whisk together the egg yolks, the whole egg, the grated cheese and lots of cracked black pepper until a thick paste forms.

  2. Render the Fat

    Place the guanciale in a pan without oil.

    Cook on medium heat until the pork is golden and crispy.

    Important: Turn off the heat and let the pan cool down slightly, so you don't scramble the eggs later.

  3. Cook the Pasta

    Put the pasta into boiling water. Cook until al dente (usually 1-2 minutes before the package instructions suggest.

    Before draining, save about a cup of the pasta water.

  4. Emulsify

    Transfer the hot pasta directly into the pan with the guanciale and its rendered fat. Toss around well.

    Turn the heat off and pour the egg and cheese mixture over the pasta.

    Toss and stir vigorously. Add a splash of the pasta water. The residual heat of the pasta will cook the eggs into a creamy sauce.

  5. Serve:

    Toss until the sauce reaches a smooth texture. If it'S too thick, add another splash of water.

    Plate and finish with Pecorino Romano, black pepper and a touch of basil.
