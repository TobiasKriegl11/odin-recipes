Mapo Tofu

Mapo Tofu is a popular Chinese dish originating from the Sichuan region.

It is seasoned with the signature spice - the Sichuan Peppercorn. This gives the dish a prominent spicy, almost numbing effect.

The dish features Tofu -of course- and is served spicy and red sauce coming from the peppercorns.

Generally it is served with either pork or ground beef.

The flavor profile is umami, salty and hot, very hot.

Ingredients

Main Components

  • Tofu: 400g
  • Ground beef or pork: 125g
  • Doubanjiang: 2 tbsp of spicy fermented bean paste
  • Douchi: 1 tbsp of fermented black beans

Aromatics & Seasonings

  • Sichuan Peppercorns: 2 tbsp, grounded into a powder
  • Ginger: 1 tbsp, finely minced
  • Garlic: 1 tbsp, finely minced
  • Scallions: 2 stalks, whites and greens separated and chopped
  • Chili Oil: to taste
  • Soy sauce: to taste
  • Liquid: 1 cup chicken stock or water

Thickener

  • Cornstarch paste: 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water
