Chickenbowl

A chicken bowl is a versatile, nutrient-dense meal featuring sliced or diced chicken.

It is served over a base of grains or fresh greens.

Typically it includes a variety of colorful vegetables, like carrots, cabbage or tomaotes.

Often it is garnished with a sauce, seeds and nuts.

Ingredients

  • Chicken:200g of sliced chicken thigh or breast.
  • Cabbage:100-150g of finely slice cabbage.
  • Carrots:70-100g of shredded raw carrots.
  • Lettuce:25-50g of lamb's lettuce.
  • Tomatoes:10-50g of small tomatoes.
  • Lemon:One lemon wedge.
  • Garnish:Sesame seeds and crushed nuts.

Steps

  1. Prepare Vegetables:

    Finely slice or shred the cabagge, carrots and lettuce. Cut the tomatoes in half.

  2. Cook Chicken:

    Sear the chicken until golden-brown and cooked through.

  3. Slice the Chicken:

    Pretty self-explanatory.

  4. Assemble:

    Create a base of cabbage and lettuce. Top with the shredded carrots.

    Place the sliced chicken alongside the vegetables.

  5. Garnish:

    Add the lemon wedge and tomatoes to the side.

    Add crushed nuts and sprinkle the chicken with sesame seeds.

Enjoy!
